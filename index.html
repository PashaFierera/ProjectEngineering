About This Project

Engineering Enablement Through Workforce Intelligence

Engineering Futures was designed as an interactive workforce intelligence report to explore how engineering skills are evolving across disciplines and countries.

Rather than simply presenting statistics, the report transforms workforce signals into actionable learning priorities that can support engineers, organizations, universities, and learning teams.
